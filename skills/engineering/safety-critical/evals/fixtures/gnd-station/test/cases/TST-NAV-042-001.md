## TST-NAV-042-001: VOR Bearing Normal Range — Cardinal Points

@test    TST-NAV-042-001
@req     HLR-NAV-042
@type    Normal Range
@al      3
@status  BASELINED
@env     Host

**Objective**: Verify bearing calculation accuracy at cardinal points under normal SNR.

**Pre-conditions**: Test signal generator configured; SNR set to 30 dB; station declination 0.0°.

| Step | Input Bearing (°) | SNR (dB) | Expected Output (°) | Tolerance |
|------|-------------------|----------|---------------------|-----------|
| 1    | 0.0               | 30       | 0.0                 | ±0.1°     |
| 2    | 90.0              | 30       | 90.0                | ±0.1°     |
| 3    | 180.0             | 30       | 180.0               | ±0.1°     |
| 4    | 270.0             | 30       | 270.0               | ±0.1°     |

**Pass/Fail Criteria**: All calculated bearings within ±0.1° of expected. Output in
range [0.0, 360.0). Return status VOR_OK.

**Boundary conditions** (HLR-NAV-042 boundary conditions — cardinal points alone do not
exercise these):

| Step | Input Bearing (°) | SNR (dB) | Expected Output (°) | Tolerance |
|------|-------------------|----------|---------------------|-----------|
| 5    | 359.95            | 30       | 0.0 (wraps)         | ±0.1°     |
| 6    | 45.0              | 20.0     | 45.0 (exact SNR gate accepted) | ±0.1° |

**Pass/Fail Criteria (boundary)**: Step 5 wraps across the 360°/0° boundary and reports
in range [0.0, 360.0), not 360.0° itself. Step 6 confirms accuracy holds at the SNR
threshold exactly (20.0 dB is accepted per HLR-NAV-043; this case checks that acceptance
also meets the ±0.1° accuracy tolerance, not just that it is accepted).

**Timing conditions** (HLR-NAV-042 tolerances — not exercised by any of the above):

| Step | Condition | Expected |
|------|-----------|----------|
| 7    | Sample-to-output latency, 30 Hz input at SNR 30 dB | ≤ 100 ms |
| 8    | Input update rate held at 29–31 Hz for 10 consecutive cycles | Output tracks at 30 Hz ± 1 Hz, no missed or duplicated cycles |

**Pass/Fail Criteria (timing)**: Step 7 measured latency does not exceed 100 ms. Step 8
confirms the output update rate stays within the ±1 Hz tolerance across sustained
operation, not just a single sample.
