## TST-NAV-042-001: VOR Bearing Normal Range — Cardinal Points

@test    TST-NAV-042-001
@req     HLR-NAV-042
@type    Normal Range
@al      3
@status  BASELINED
@env     Host

**Objective**: Verify bearing calculation accuracy at cardinal points under normal SNR.

**Pre-conditions**: Test signal generator configured; SNR set to 30 dB; station declination 0.0°.

| Step | Input Bearing (°) | SNR (dB) | Expected Output (°) | Tolerance |
|------|-------------------|----------|---------------------|-----------|
| 1    | 0.0               | 30       | 0.0                 | ±0.1°     |
| 2    | 90.0              | 30       | 90.0                | ±0.1°     |
| 3    | 180.0             | 30       | 180.0               | ±0.1°     |
| 4    | 270.0             | 30       | 270.0               | ±0.1°     |

**Pass/Fail Criteria**: All calculated bearings within ±0.1° of expected. Output in
range [0.0, 360.0). Return status VOR_OK.
